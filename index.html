Inside the shop

Marble floors, low light, no rush.

Blade Co. opened in 2026 on Band Khanna Road, Khurram Muslim Colony, built for boys who want a proper cut without the wait. Chairs face the street, the back room stays quiet for shaves and beard work, and every visit ends with a hot towel finish.

We keep late hours on purpose. If you're coming straight from work or finishing the night out, the chair is still open.
